# Naming shortlist and trademark notes

Last reviewed: **November 18, 2026**. Trademark statuses below are observations
from that date, not a continuing availability guarantee.

## Current decision

Continue developing and distributing the project as **Primeboard**, while
actively exploring a more distinctive long-term name. No replacement has been
selected. Keep this shortlist open to new candidates, and revisit the name before
investing substantially in commercial hardware, packaging, or a broader launch.

The shortlist ranks the three leading candidates from the naming discussion.
It combines product fit with preliminary U.S. trademark and web findings; none
has received legal clearance. Axiochrom is a spelling variant of Axiochrome,
rather than a fourth candidate.

| Rank | Candidate | Product fit | Main unresolved issue | Position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Axiochrome** | Axiom + chromatic: mathematics, musical scales, and color | Active **AXICHROM** registration and laboratory product family | Leading candidate for further review |
| 2 | **Axiotone** | Axiom + tone: mathematics and sound | Active **AXITONE** registration and industrial fan products | Strong alternative for further review |
| 3 | **Spectrilo** | Suggests spectra and trills | Active **SPECTRIO** registrations, including music and digital-signage software | Reserve candidate; materially greater concern from related software |

This ordering is a naming judgment, not a legal risk score. In particular,
Axiochrome has not been established as legally safer than Axiotone.

## Axiochrome

**Naming assessment:** the strongest conceptual fit for an instrument combining
mathematical structures, sound, and visualizations. Suggested pronunciation:
“AK-see-oh-krohm.” The final **e** makes the word look complete and makes the
color connection easier to recognize. Axiochrom feels more like a scientific
product name and could lead people to add the missing **e** when searching.
Either spelling emphasizes the visual side more immediately than music.

**Federal search:** no exact Axiochrome or Axiochrom record appeared in the
combined search, which included both spellings and close variants.

| Existing mark | Status observed | Record and coverage | Significance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AXICHROM** | Live, registered | Cytiva Sweden AB; serial **77366193**; class **009**, laboratory chromatography columns and associated components. [USPTO record](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=77366193&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch) | Very similar spelling and sound. The stated goods differ substantially from a musical instrument, but this is the main match requiring review. |
| **AXICHROME** | Dead, abandoned | Howden North America Inc.; serial **77881895**; class **011**, commercial and industrial ventilation fans. [USPTO record](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=77881895&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch) | Another close spelling. An abandoned application does not establish that all use or rights have ended. |

Cytiva actively documents **AxiChrom** as a chromatography product family. Its
systems also use control equipment and software for laboratory workflows, so
review should consider the actual product ecosystem rather than assuming that
all software is unrelated. This is not evidence that Cytiva offers musical
software. [Cytiva product documentation](https://cdn.cytivalifesciences.com/api/public/content/WX_pY6WmUE2lFFE13cHOng-pdf)

The exact spelling **AxioChrom** also appears in a
[nanoparticle patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20210170049A1/en), and
**Axiochrom** appears as a microscope description in a
[research paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2132472/). These are
additional search leads, not verified evidence of separate current brands;
spelling errors or variants are possible.

**Open questions:** assess AXICHROM and any continued AXICHROME use against the
planned software and hardware. Check phonetic variants, music products, app
stores, repositories, domains, and relevant non-U.S. markets. Choosing
Axiochrome instead of Axiochrom should be a branding choice, not a presumed
way around another mark.

## Axiotone

**Naming assessment:** a clear mathematics-and-music connection. It describes
the sound aspect more immediately than Axiochrome, but says less about color
and visualization. It remains a strong alternative.

**Federal search:** no exact Axiotone record was found. The principal close
match is **AXITONE**, live and registered to the ebm-papst Mulfingen business,
serial **97192783**, in classes **007** and **011**. Its goods cover electric
motors, industrial fans, ventilation equipment, and components.
[USPTO record](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=97192783&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch)

This is an actual product name, not only a database entry: ebm-papst markets
**AxiTone** fans for quiet ventilation and heat-pump applications. The extra
**o** in Axiotone leaves a close visual and phonetic resemblance, although the
identified goods differ substantially from musical software and keyboards.
[Manufacturer product announcement](https://www.ebmpapst.com/at/en/newsroom/news/2023/axitone.html)

## Spectrilo

**Naming assessment:** an expressive name suggesting spectra and trills, with
room for both music and visuals. It remains third because the closest identified
brand operates in a more relevant software market.

**Federal search:** no exact Spectrilo record was found. The important close
match is **SPECTRIO**, owned by Spectrio LLC:

| Serial | Status observed | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [88937815](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=88937815&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch) | Live, registered; registration **6,227,273** | Class **042**: hosted software for managing business music, messaging, digital signage, and marketing content. |
| [88937805](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=88937805&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch) | Live, registered | Class **038**: internet-based voice communications. |
| [85396276](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=85396276&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch) | Live, registered | Class **035**: advertising services. |

[Spectrio's current business](https://www.spectrio.com/) includes audiovisual
content and digital-signage services. Spectrilo differs from Spectrio by the
insertion of a single **l**. Although Primeboard's creative instrument has a
different purpose from business marketing software, the combination of name
similarity and music/visual software warrants a focused legal review.

Older SPECTRIO records, including serials **85396278** and **85396265**, were
cancelled; that does not negate the live registrations above. The expanded
search also returned **SPECTRIX**, serial **88666239**, live and registered for
sensing and lighting technology in classes 009, 011, and 042, and multiple
SPECTRIS marks. These are secondary leads; SPECTRIO is the clearest concern
identified so far.
[SPECTRIX record](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=88666239&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch)

The exact word **Spectrilo** was also found as a fan-created creature name in
online discussions. This is not an official Pokemon brand finding or proof of
a commercial music-software mark, but it means the word is not wholly unused
online. [Fan-project discussion](https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/fakemon-central-read-the-rules-in-the-op.3565414/page-3)

**Open questions:** have the SPECTRIO comparison assessed before advancing this
candidate. If it remains viable, investigate common-law use and other markets.
Do not treat the absence of an exact Spectrilo registration as clearance.

## Why Primeboard remains under review

The current name has two concrete issues worth retaining with this decision:

- **PRIME BOARD**, serial **90715732**, registration **6,903,176**, was observed
  live and registered on the **Supplemental Register**, registered November 15,
  2022. Classes **009** and **028** cover physical math-learning boards and chips
  used to explore number properties and patterns. The record claims first use
  on October 13, 2021, and first commercial use on June 6, 2022. Its wording is
  in standard characters. The shared mathematical/educational context merits
  review; joining the words into “Primeboard” does not remove their similar
  sound and meaning. Supplemental registration should not be treated as an
  absence of rights. [USPTO record](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=90715732&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch)
- **Primeboard** is also used for interactive classroom and business displays
  at [primeboard.eu](https://www.primeboard.eu/). This is evidence of marketplace
  use, not a determination of the owner's U.S. or international trademark rights.

Keeping the name for now is a project decision, not a prediction that no
complaint will arrive. A naming dispute primarily raises trademark questions,
which are distinct from copyright claims.
[USPTO explanation of trademark and copyright](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/trademark-patent-copyright)

## Search scope and interpretation

This is a preliminary U.S. federal-record and public-web screen, not an
exhaustive clearance search. Statuses and goods were inspected in the official
[USPTO search system](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/); the linked TSDR records allow
later review. Exact-name searches covered Spectrilo and Axiotone, with live and
dead records included. The following broader queries were also run in the
field-tag search mode:

```text
CM:(axiochrom* OR axichrom* OR axiokrom* OR "axio chrome" OR "axio chrom")
CM:(axiot* OR axiat* OR axiton* OR axyton* OR "axio tone")
CM:(spectril* OR spectri* OR spektril*)
```

The first two returned 2 and 16 records respectively, which were reviewed. The
Spectrilo query returned 77 records; the material matches above were inspected,
but the entire result set was not exhaustively analyzed. The Axiochrome query
includes both proposed exact spellings; its only results were AXICHROM and
AXICHROME.

No exact-name result means only that the searches did not return one. Similar
names, unregistered use, related goods, and other jurisdictions can still matter.
Likewise, cancellation or abandonment of a federal record is not proof that
the name is free to use. Class numbers organize the records; they do not by
themselves decide whether products are related. The relevant comparison includes
sound, appearance, meaning, commercial impression, and the goods or services.
[USPTO likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)

Domain availability, social handles, app stores, state registrations, full
common-law use, and international clearance remain open. The preliminary
ranking does not resolve those checks.

## Continuing the naming work

- Keep **Primeboard** as the current name while developing the instrument.
- Keep seeking names that are distinctive, easy to pronounce and spell, and
  broad enough for mathematics, sound, color, and future physical instruments.
- Add new candidates here with dated findings and source links; replace a
  weaker shortlisted candidate when a better one emerges.
- Treat **Axiochrom** as an alternate spelling under Axiochrome. **Axiomagic**
  was set aside after the project owner identified an existing open-source
  project with that name; no trademark clearance conclusion was reached for it.
- Before committing to a replacement, complete the outstanding marketplace,
  domain, and jurisdiction checks and obtain a focused trademark review of the
  intended software and hardware offering.
- Once a replacement is selected, plan the repository, domain, application,
  installer, release, and documentation migration together.
