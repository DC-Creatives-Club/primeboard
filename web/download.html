Before you install

These installers aren’t publisher-signed, so Windows or macOS may show a security warning. On Mac, after the first blocked launch, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Only approve an installer you downloaded here.

The app works without an internet connection. Updates are installed manually; your saved recordings and settings stay on your computer.
