# Eigenmode views

The 26 primes keep the same integer exponents, keyboard letters, optical hues,
and musical pitches in every view. The exact number is optional in the modal
view: open **Prime encoding** to inspect or copy it. It encodes mode weights;
it does not encode phase or the current animation time.

## Spatial basis

Use the unit square with simply supported edges (zero displacement and bending
moment), for an ideal thin, homogeneous, isotropic Kirchhoff–Love plate. The
ordinary shapes are sin(m pi x) sin(n pi y); natural angular frequency is
proportional to m²+n². Boundary conditions matter: this is not a free-edge plate
held at its center, and we do not simulate granular dynamics.

Our orthonormal basis is:

    phi_mm = 2 sin(m pi x) sin(m pi y)
    phi_mn,+/- = sqrt(2) [sin(m pi x) sin(n pi y)
                         +/- sin(n pi x) sin(m pi y)], m < n

The first 26 functions are ordered by m²+n², then m, then + before −. The
rotation within each degenerate pair is a valid eigenbasis and produces the
curved and diagonal nodal patterns. Each shape has integral phi² dx dy = 1.

Reference for the plate equation, boundary conditions and sine-product solution:
[Jim Woodhouse, Euphonics, Plate vibration](https://euphonics.org/3-2-3-plate-vibration/).
The implementation and these derivations are original; no reference images or
source code are used.

## Three readouts

**Colored nodes** draws a narrow band around each individual mode's zero set,
then adds those layers. This intentionally preserves the individual patterns.
It is a diagram of which modes are present, not the nodal set of their summed
displacement or a prediction of where sand will settle. Line thickness uses
an approximate distance |phi| / max(2, |gradient phi|), with Gaussian width
0.005 of the plate side. Each layer's weight is e_i.

Hues come from each key's CIE XYZ, normalized by its largest positive linear
sRGB channel for visibility. Weighted XYZ layers add before the existing
gamut clipping, exposure compression and sRGB transfer. Thus the palette is
related to the light view, but normalized layer brightness is illustrative.

**Vibration strength** shows mean squared displacement, not local mechanical
energy. With distinct musical frequencies and fixed amplitudes,

    u(x,y,t) = sum sqrt(e_i) phi_i(x,y) cos(2 pi f_i t)
    long-time mean of u² = (1/2) sum e_i phi_i(x,y)²

The spatial integral is sum e_i / 2. It is bright at antinodes and dark where
motion is small. If natural plate frequencies were used instead, equal-frequency
degenerate modes would retain coherent cross terms; this formula would need
those terms or an explicit incoherence assumption. Physical energy would also
require velocities and plate-curvature terms. We make neither claim here.

**Moving field** shows the signed linear sum u, with blue for positive and
orange for negative displacement. Frequencies are the same distinct
equal-tempered pitches as audio, slowed by a common factor of 200 for viewing.
These are musically retuned plate shapes, not a solution of the unmodified
plate's free-vibration equation. Relative frequency ratios are preserved;
visual phase is illustrative, not locked to audio oscillator phase. Most
equal-tempered chords are not exactly periodic. The animation can be frozen,
defaults to frozen when reduced motion is requested, and stops for a paused
song or a hidden page.

For a fixed instant, nodal lines of the sum can arise from cancellation even
where neither individual mode is zero. For a point to remain a node for all
time under distinct nonzero frequencies, every active mode must vanish there.
That distinction is why the three readouts look different for a chord.
