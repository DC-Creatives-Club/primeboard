Branches Return
==============
Original composition by Codex (OpenAI's AI coding assistant), November 2026.
Written for Primeboard at the user's request. This is a newly composed prefix
score, not an arrangement or transcription of an existing song.

Setting: Cantor algebra, Union.
Meter: 3/4. Tempo: quarter note = 96. Length: 16 bars / 30 seconds.
The score and event encoding are included under the repository's MIT license.
No samples or third-party recordings are used.

The opening motif uses 00, 01, and 10 (440, 550, and 660 Hz) in sequence.
00 with 10 makes an open fifth. Adding 01 joins the sibling cylinders 00 and
01 into 0, lowering that voice from 440 to 220 Hz while 10 stays at 660 Hz.

Bars 5-8 answer in the next octave. The chord {000,010,100} sounds at
880, 1100, and 1320 Hz, a pure 4:5:6 major triad. These cylinders neither
contain one another nor form sibling pairs, so their union keeps three tones.
The middle passages contrast this stable chord with controlled downward merges.

The last bar holds 00, then adds 01, then 1. Its union descends through
C(00), C(0), and the whole Cantor set C: 440 -> 220 -> 110 Hz. The root is
reached only at this final cadence, without pressing the empty-prefix key.
A short rest follows. The frequency mapping is unchanged throughout.

for their written durations, which is essential for the merging gestures.
