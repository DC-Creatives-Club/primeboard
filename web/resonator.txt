DRIVEN EIGENMODES

Each held key continuously drives its labeled mode at its audible resonance.
A fresh key has driver weight w = 1. Holding keeps that force constant: the
oscillator supplies the buildup toward equilibrium, rather than increasing
input strength with elapsed time. Sustain retains the driver. Replaying a
sustained key adds one unit of weight, up to 32. Release removes the force;
the existing motion rings down. Clear resets sound and display immediately.

For frequency ratio r_i = f_i/f_1 and omega_i = 2 pi f_i, the solver is

    q_i'' + 2 gamma_i q_i' + omega_i^2 q_i = F_i(t)
    F_i(t) = 2 gamma_i omega_i sqrt(w_i)/r_i cos(omega_i t)
    gamma_i = 4 + 1.5 sqrt(r_i) per second

It advances the exact damped homogeneous solution plus the resonant particular
solution over each time step. Changes to the driver preserve displacement and
velocity. At equilibrium, displacement amplitude is sqrt(w_i)/r_i. Audio
samples the sum of r_i q_i, followed by compression, limiting, and master gain.
Damping and loudness balance are illustrative, not calibrated material data.
The impulse solver remains available internally for tests; the app uses drive.

The display solves the same oscillator at the same frequencies, including
any selected audible sphere octave reduction, and extracts its envelope:

    A_i = r_i sqrt(q_i^2 + (q_i'/omega_i)^2)
    displayed field = sum A_i phi_i

The key’s × readout shows A_i^2 while its driver is selected. A fresh held key
approaches ×1.0; its button unpresses immediately on release while the field
and sound ring down. Number instead uses integer prime exponents. Cantor
continues to sound fixed-strength tones for the simplified set.

Colored nodes uses A_i to brighten each mode’s nodal pattern. Mode mixture
shows the envelope-weighted signed sum; Vibration strength shows its square.
The cube/sphere normalize contour fields for readability, with brightness
following the envelope. Their meshes stay fixed. These are inspection views,
not instantaneous displacement or slow-motion footage. Frozen fields preview
selected steady amplitudes. Sand uses the same envelopes for its approximate
agitation field; it freezes particle transport when requested.

All physical views use this interaction. The simply supported plate and scalar
surfaces retain their illustrative frequency mappings. Free plate and elastic
shells retain their computed pitches. No extra modes, noise, contact forces,
acoustic radiation, or room response are introduced.

Recordings store driver weights rather than sampled response envelopes. The
same oscillator supplies buildup and release during playback and offline
export, so a recorded hold is not enveloped twice. Older takes retain their
stored varying strengths as driver weights.
